02 / WHAT THE WORK CAN INCLUDE

Capabilities, not a preset package.

I bring together only the work the business actually needs. Scope is agreed before we begin, then organized around three outcomes: get found, make the value clear, and make the next step easy.

01 / FIND

Get discovered.

Build visibility where people already look for answers and recommendations.

  • Search and Maps
  • AI search readiness
  • Paid discovery
  • App Store visibility when relevant
02 / UNDERSTAND

Make the value clear.

Help the right person quickly understand what the business does and why it is the right fit.

  • Positioning and messaging
  • Website strategy, design, and build
  • Brand systems
  • Proof and content
03 / CHOOSE

Make the next step easy.

Remove friction between interest and action, then make follow-up easier to manage.

  • Conversion paths
  • Booking and contact
  • Lead capture and follow-up
  • Measurement and reporting
