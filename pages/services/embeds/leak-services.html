LEAK 01

Overlapping software

You are paying three tools to do one job. Your last audit was your accountant noticing the renewals, not your team noticing the waste.

LEAK 02

Attribution blindness

You can tell me revenue is up. You cannot tell me which channel earned it. So you keep funding what you always funded.

LEAK 03

ASO neglect

Your app is downloaded by people who already knew about you. The App Store is a search engine you do not show up in.

LEAK 04

Channel mis-spend

You spend the most where the conversion is the slowest. The dashboards do not tell you because they were not built to.
